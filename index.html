Business Services

We provide professional business trade agency and local support services for international clients.

Our services include supplier sourcing, product procurement, supplier communication, factory visits, factory inspection, product inspection, business visits, logistics coordination, translation, interpretation and other customized business support.

We assist clients in identifying suitable suppliers, communicating with manufacturers, coordinating product requirements and supporting business activities.

We also provide local assistance for clients who need reliable support during business visits, purchasing, sourcing and other trade-related activities.
